About My Journey

A genuine look at where I am today and the principles guiding my technical development.

I am a prospective Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) Artificial Intelligence student. My goal is to develop into a well-rounded Machine Learning Engineer and Software Developer capable of designing reliable algorithms and deploying resilient production systems.

Rather than jumping straight into high-level AI abstractions and wrapper scripts, I believe in taking the time to understand the mathematical intuition, memory models, and algorithmic foundations that power modern intelligent computing.
